Finite-state morphological parser

MorphWalk

Paste English text. Every word is run through a transducer that returns its lemma, category, and inflection. When a word has more than one reading, all of them are kept.

  • more than one reading
  • not in the lexicon
  • closed class

A finite-state transducer: lexicon, morphotactics, and English spelling rules. Out-of-vocabulary names stay unanalyzed.
